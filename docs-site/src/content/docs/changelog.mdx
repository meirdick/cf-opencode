## 0.1.0

First release. It has one package, `cf-opencode`: the CLI. It carries the Worker source and the workspace image. It deploys them to your Cloudflare account with `cf-opencode init`.

What works:

- Workspaces on Cloudflare Containers. They pause to a snapshot when idle and restore on connect.
- Models through the AI binding of your account and AI Gateway. Browsing through the Browser Run binding. No keys.
- `shell`, `ssh` and `ssh-config` over the Worker. No open port.
- GitHub access with a token that stays in the Worker. `init` sets it up once from the `gh` login of the machine.
- `connect --continue` and `connect --session` open a session in the workspace. `sessions` lists sessions across workspaces.
- `rebuild` moves a workspace to the deployed image. Snapshots refresh before they expire.

Known limits are on the [Limits and costs](/limits/) page.
